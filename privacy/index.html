Confetto

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Confetto collects no data. None. Not a single byte leaves your Mac.

We built Confetto to throw confetti, not to learn anything about you. There is no account to create, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

What stays on your Mac

Your confetti presets, keyboard shortcuts, schedules, and preferences are stored locally on your device using the standard macOS preferences system. We never see them, and they are never transmitted anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No data collection or tracking of any kind
  • No network connections — the app has no internet access
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No access to your files, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • No screen recording or keystroke logging

Sandboxing

Confetto is distributed through the Mac App Store and runs inside Apple's App Sandbox. It requests no special permissions or entitlements beyond the sandbox itself. The confetti you see is drawn on a transparent, click-through overlay window; it captures no input and no screen content.

Children's privacy

Confetto is suitable for all ages and, because it collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app collects no data, we do not anticipate meaningful changes.
